# Claude Certified Architect – Professional (CCAR-P) prep

A practice coach for CCAR-P, packaged as a Claude skill: `ccar-p-coach/`.

From **your own copy of the official exam guide** it runs a 30-item baseline, builds a plan
around your date and hours, drills a domain, runs timed mocks, and ranks your weak spots, with
the reasoning and trap behind every answer. It contains no exam questions.

- **Guide:** download it from the CCAR-P page on the Anthropic Partner Academy (not included).
- **Install:** add the folder as a skill in Claude, or copy it to `~/.claude/skills/ccar-p-coach/`.
- **Use:** attach the guide, say *"Start CCAR-P prep"*, then `baseline`, `plan`, `drill`, `mock`, `review`.

The Professional exam leans on architecture trade-offs, governance and stakeholder
communication, so expect scenario answers that weigh cost, risk and people, not just code.

Unofficial community material; the official guide is the authority.
